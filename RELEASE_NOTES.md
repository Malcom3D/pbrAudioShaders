# pbrAudioRender
Welcome to version v0.3.0 of `pbrAudioRender`! This major update brings a complete architectural overhaul focusing on performance, advanced acoustic simulation engines, and enhanced Blender integration.

* **New TUI-based DAW:** Introducing `pbrAudioDAW`, bringing a streamlined Text User Interface for managing your audio workflows.
* **Enhanced Blender Integration:** Enjoy brand-new 3D viewport widgets, a dedicated Viewport Panel, and advanced collision/render exporter tools.
* **Advanced Acoustic Engines:** New `pbrAudioFDTD` and `pbrAudioRay` simulation engines provide state-of-the-art wave propagation, ray tracing, and multiple output formats including Ambisonics, Surround, Stereo, and Stereo with HRTF.
* **Performance & Stability:** The storage and physics engines have been heavily optimized with parallel processing and mmapped modes to prevent out-of-memory errors and handle large-scale modal synthesis seamlessly.
